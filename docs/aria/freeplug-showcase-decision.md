# Showcase gallery location — standing decision (Oct 15, 2026, Cheke)

- The showcase gallery of local-business website templates lives on **freeplug-in.dev**, not 72after.dev.
- 72after.dev gets only a small "See our work" section on its homepage linking to the Freeplug showcase.
- Why: Freeplug is where local businesses sign up, pick a template, and pay, so showing designs there converts visitors. The showcase shares the same template list as the picker, which already ensures no two customers get the same template.
- Mix: roughly half the showcase uses different templates, half the same, but no two customers share one.
- 72After stays the consulting brand.
- Building the Freeplug showcase is a medium change: Aria and Lina pick it up when work resumes Saturday, Oct 17.
