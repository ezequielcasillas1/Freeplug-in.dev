# Outdoor Freeplug doormat

Blank: Printify Doormat 1346; M.i.A Merchandise 80; variant 103043. One available size: 24 × 16 inches. Grade A tufted coir coconut fiber, black vinyl backing, assembled in the USA from globally sourced parts. Public provider page lists New Hampshire US fulfillment.

All three artwork PNGs are transparent, exact API print area 7275 × 4875 px at 300 DPI; matching outlined SVG sources and drawn mockups included. No embedded fonts. Options 01/02 are charcoal only for reliable contrast; option 03 adds brand red #ba3d3d. The coir provides the natural ground; there is no ivory/white ink layer. The circle in option 02 is a bold outline with the real FP mark, avoiding a white-ink knockout assumption.

Printify's API calls this decoration dye-sublimation even though the blank is coir; this handoff preserves that metadata rather than asserting a different process. The API canvas is slightly larger than the nominal 7200 × 4800 product face. Do not crop away that extra 75px or resize to nominal dimensions: confirm trim guides later in the Product Creator. All foreground has at least 300px quiet margin; backgrounds remain transparent.

Outdoor production caveats: coir is coarse and varies naturally. Color detail/edge fidelity will be softer than a screen preview; black/charcoal options recommended over red for maximum legibility. Prefer a covered entryway, avoid standing water/prolonged soaking, and confirm manufacturer care instructions before publishing claims. Coir can shed and ink can fade/wear; not a guaranteed all-weather industrial mat. Vinyl backing is not rubber and no anti-slip certification is asserted.

Mockup texture and mat silhouettes are drawn/generated locally, not stock product photos. Physical sample and product template alignment review are required before sale. No upload, product creation, purchase or publication performed.
