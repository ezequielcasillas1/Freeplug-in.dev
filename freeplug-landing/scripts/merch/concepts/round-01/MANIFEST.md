# Freeplug Concepts — Round 01

Cheke OK: "go aheading" (t122u), Nov 9, 2026 ~10:11 AM CT. Local files only. No Printify / site.

## Groups (3 × 6 = 18)
1. **business-client-sample** — made-up local businesses for sample shirt pitches. No real names/marks. Optional tiny Freeplug credit only.
2. **freeplug-brand-merch** — Freeplug public merch for customers.
3. **freeplug-crew** — shirts for the Freeplug team (CREW / TEAM PLUG).

## Themes covered
plug-pun, faith-kjv (exact KJV wording), san-marcos, street-art, retro, minimal — spread across groups.

## Contrast
`-light-garment.png` = dark ink → light blanks only. `-dark-garment.png` = ivory/light ink → dark blanks only. See `/workspace/logos/freeplug/merch/CONTRAST_MAP.json`.

## Krea
Illustration assets generated with `krea/krea-2/medium-turbo`. Exact wording composited in Pillow with FP Extended, Freeplug Dispatch, FP Cursive. Credits: ~5081.48 before / ~5059.48 after (~22 CU for 8 illustration gens).
