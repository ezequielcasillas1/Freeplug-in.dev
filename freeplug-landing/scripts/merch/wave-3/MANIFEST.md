## Contrast / blank-color rule (Cheke Nov 8, 2026)

**Dark or black text/art must never go on a black or dark blank.**

- `*-light-garment.png` = dark ink (charcoal/black + red). **Allowed blanks:** white, ivory, natural, cream, light ash, heather, sand, and other light colors. **NEVER** black, charcoal, navy, dark heather, or other dark blanks.
- `*-dark-garment.png` = light ink (ivory + red, red often ivory-outlined). **Allowed blanks:** black, charcoal, navy, dark heather, and other dark colors. **NEVER** white/light blanks.
- Embroidery pairs follow the same rule (ivory thread on dark blanks; black thread on light blanks).
- Brand red on dark blanks gets an ivory outline where needed for contrast.
- Original unscoped `front.png` / `back.png` files remain as source references; **use the `-light-garment` / `-dark-garment` files for production.**

See `/workspace/logos/freeplug/merch/CONTRAST_MAP.json` for the machine-readable map.
